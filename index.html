FIRST TIME HERE?

The Essential Guide.

Gotokuji Temple is located in Setagaya, Tokyo. Gotokuji Station on the Odakyu Line and Miyanosaka Station on the Tokyu Setagaya Line are useful starting points.

Before you visit

Gotokuji is an active Buddhist temple. Please respect posted rules, local residents and other visitors. Check the official temple website for current opening hours, access restrictions and visitor guidance.
